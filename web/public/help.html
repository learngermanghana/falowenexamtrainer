Is the class hybrid?

Yes, our classes are hybrid: you can attend online or in person. Recorded lectures are also available if you miss a class.

A1 exam registration is currently GHS 3,000, separate from the class tuition fee. For A2–C2, check the Goethe-Institut Ghana website for the current fee. Please confirm the latest official charge and registration availability before paying.

How long is the course?

Each level can be completed in 10 weeks, and you pay tuition separately for each level. Your contract lasts six months in total, allowing three months of free catch-up time within that period if travel or illness interrupts your studies.

View course details

Can I learn up to B1 or B2?

Each German level takes 10 weeks, and tuition is paid separately for each level. You can study up to your preferred level, such as B1 or B2. Falowen provides mock exam samples to practise and check your progress, so you do not need to take an official exam after every level as part of our learning pathway. You can take the official exam at your chosen target level. Official exam registration is paid separately.

Can I join late?

You can join a class only while it has completed fewer than four sessions (0–3 sessions). You will need to use the recorded lectures to catch up. Contact our team to confirm the current session count before enrolling.

Tuition includes your classes, recorded lectures and access to the Falowen app, including your digital books and learning materials. Physical books can be requested separately through the book page if preferred. Official exam registration is not included in tuition.

What you see in the app

To continue lessons, tap or click Course Book. On mobile, Course Book is the first item in the bottom navigation. On desktop, Course Book appears in the campus navigation row.

Course Book opens your lessons. Music opens German learning songs as a main mobile destination. Vocab opens vocabulary browse, flashcards and quizzes; on mobile, open More → Vocab. Results opens your scores and feedback.

Do not look for My Library, Learning Hub, or My Hub. These are not current Falowen navigation labels.

Free trial, payment and access

  • A new learner can activate one one-time 7-day free trial.
  • Starting the trial does not count as a tuition payment or reduce the tuition balance.
  • After the trial ends, progress and scores are retained for 30 days so the learner can pay and continue with the same student code and progress.
  • Use Account → Billing for tuition, balance, payment history and available receipts.

Course Book and assignments

The normal flow is Course Book → current lesson → Falowen Radio when required → Learn/Grammar → workbook → Submit when required → Results after marking.

  • If Falowen Radio appears first, complete it before continuing.
  • Self-practice should not be submitted unless the lesson explicitly identifies it as a teacher-marked assignment.
  • Student submission lives inside the relevant teacher-marked workbook's Submit tab. There is no separate general student submission page.
  • If a workbook has no Submit tab, do not assume it needs tutor submission.

Levels

A1: mixes foundation lessons, self-practice and teacher-marked assignments.

A2 and B1: use grammar/learning material, workbook tasks and teacher-marked submissions.

B2 and C1: are self-learning tracks with AI support. Exam File and Date is available in the Exams Room; standard class Attendance and Class Members remain hidden.

C2: uses advanced self-learning and AI-supported practice. Do not assume a teacher-marked Submit requirement unless the current page explicitly provides one.

Common things students want to do

  • Continue a lesson: Course Book → current lesson.
  • Find homework: Course Book → current lesson → workbook. Complete Falowen Radio first when required.
  • Submit work: use the Submit tab inside the relevant teacher-marked workbook.
  • See a correction: open Results.
  • Practise for an exam: open Exams Room.
  • Get a receipt: open Account → Billing.
  • Move to the next level: open Account → Upgrade.

Results, attendance and exams

Results: /campus/results — marked work, scores, feedback and progress. Teacher-marked course assignments use a standard 60% pass threshold.

Attendance: /campus/attendance — attendance rate plus present, absent and pending sessions. The page can generate an Attendance Record / Attendance Transcript PDF.

Exam File and Date: /exams/file — Goethe/exam information inside the Exams Room.

Exams Room: /exams/overview — separate exam-style practice.

Study Calendar: /exams/study — study and exam-preparation planning.

Full mocks, sections and practice levels

Open Exams Room. Choose Start Full Mock for all four skills together, or practise Lesen, Hören, Schreiben or Sprechen individually below. The mock library lets you choose a published mock or resume one in progress. Availability varies by level.

Use the exam level selector to practise at your enrolled level or a lower level. A2 can choose A1; B1 can choose A2 or A1. Your Course Book level stays unchanged.

For course assignment scores and tutor feedback, open Results. For saved mock progress and recent exam practice results, open Exams Room.

Your live class schedule

On your signed-in homepage, use View full class schedule on the next live class card for your assigned class timetable. Public Upcoming Classes describes new classes.

Pay or continue a payment

  1. Log in and open Account → Billing. Check your balance and payment state.
  2. When online payment is available, use Pay balance or Continue payment, as shown, to open Paystack. Check the amount and displayed fees, then use a method offered by checkout.
  3. Falowen confirms payment after provider confirmation. Returning from checkout alone does not confirm payment.
  4. If payment is pending, refresh payment status and check transaction history before paying again. If it remains unresolved, contact Falowen support with your payment reference.

Account

  • Student Data — profile and student information.
  • Notifications — notification settings.
  • Billing — tuition, balance, payment history and available receipts.
  • Upgrade — next-level upgrade eligibility. Outstanding balances can block an upgrade.

If something looks wrong

  • Workbook returns to Falowen Radio: finish the required Radio step, then use Continue.
  • No Submit tab: the activity may be self-practice or not currently teacher-marked.
  • No result yet: marking or synchronization may still be pending.
  • B2/C1 learner cannot see Attendance or Class Members: those class-based tabs are intentionally hidden for self-learning tracks. Exam File and Date is available in the Exams Room.
  • Payment made but access has not updated: use the setup/account status refresh flow.
