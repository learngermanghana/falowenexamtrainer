What is included in the free listening test?

Try Falowen’s A1 Hören Sample 1, with original audio and all 15 questions. You do not need an account or payment details. Submit your answers to see your score and corrections.

  • Teil 1: six short dialogues, heard twice.
  • Teil 2: four short announcements, heard once.
  • Teil 3: five short texts, heard twice.

How to practise

  1. Choose a quiet place and check that your audio works.
  2. Read each question and its options before listening.
  3. Follow the playback instructions and choose an answer for every question.
  4. Submit, review your corrections and listen again to the details you missed.

Listen for changes, not just familiar words

A recording may mention two times or prices before correcting one. Listen to the whole sentence, particularly words such as “aber” (but), “nicht” (not) and “erst” (not until). A familiar word alone does not make an option correct.
