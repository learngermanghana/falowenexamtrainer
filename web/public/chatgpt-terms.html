Falowen Learning Companion — Terms of Use

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Falowen Learning Companion is an optional read-only connection provided by Learn Language Education Academy (Falowen) for Falowen students who want additional German learning support in ChatGPT.

Eligibility and accounts

Use your own authorised Falowen student account. Do not share access credentials, impersonate another learner, or use someone else's school account. Use of the connection is also subject to OpenAI's applicable ChatGPT terms and availability requirements.

Supplemental learning assistance

The connection may summarise saved lesson information, provide explanations, help you practise grammar or answer questions, and show available assignments, feedback and the verified next class. ChatGPT's generated responses are supplemental guidance and may be inaccurate. Always follow the official instructions, deadlines and assessed exercises in Falowen Campus.

What remains in Falowen Campus

Official coursework submissions, tutor grading, payment, student-account changes and exam attempts are not performed through this plugin. Only authorised Falowen Campus workflows may create or change those records. Falowen completion certificates are not Goethe-Institut certificates.

Privacy, access and support

Connecting is voluntary. See the Falowen Learning Companion Privacy Notice for the data that may be shared with ChatGPT and how to disconnect. Use Falowen Help for support or account issues. Broader Falowen terms, eligibility and policies are published on the Falowen legal and enrolment page.

Changes

Falowen may update the companion, its tools or these terms. Material changes will be posted on this page. The feature may be restricted, paused or discontinued as necessary for security, compliance or service operations.
