Build confidence with everyday German

A1 practice focuses on short, familiar messages: appointments, shopping, transport and introductions. Before a mock, practise finding a time, a place or a price without translating every word.

Try a quick A1 reading question

Hallo Lena, unser Deutschkurs beginnt heute um 17 Uhr, nicht um 16 Uhr. Wir treffen uns in Raum 3. Bis später! Paul

Wann beginnt der Deutschkurs heute?

  1. Um 15 Uhr.
  2. Um 16 Uhr.
  3. Um 17 Uhr.
Show answer and explanation

c) Um 17 Uhr. The message changes the start time to 17:00. “Nicht um 16 Uhr” rules out the old time. Look for corrections like “nicht” in short messages.

Prepare all four skills

  • Lesen: identify information in notices and short messages.
  • Hören: listen for key details in dialogues and announcements.
  • Schreiben: respond to every point in a short writing task.
  • Sprechen: practise an introduction, questions and polite requests aloud.
