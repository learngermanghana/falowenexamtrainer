Falowen Learning Companion — Privacy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Falowen Learning Companion connects your own Falowen student account to ChatGPT when you choose to authorize the connection. It is operated by Learn Language Education Academy (Falowen), Ghana. The connection is optional.

What the connection may access

After you sign in to Falowen and grant permission, the read-only part of the connection can access your name, authorised German and Kiswahili programme and course levels, access entitlements, learner-facing course material and vocabulary, assignment and submission status, recorded tutor feedback and scores, verified mock-exam attempts, section scores and available answer reviews, and your assigned live-class schedule, topic and class Zoom link where configured. Exam-only accounts are restricted to authorised examination tools. Personal revision and exam recommendations may use these records and an exam date you supply. These details are returned to ChatGPT only in response to relevant tool requests. It cannot view another learner's account through your connection.

Optional practice notes and vocabulary review

The normal connection uses the read-only falowen:read permission. If you separately authorize falowen:practice:write, ChatGPT may save a short note about your own learning only when you request it. These notes are attached to your authenticated Falowen student account, distinct from tutor-marked assignments and timed exams; they do not determine official grades or completion. Notes have a 90-day expiry marker, subject to the configured database-retention mechanism; to request access or deletion, contact Falowen Help. If you explicitly confirm vocabulary review outcomes, the same optional permission allows the Companion to save words and meanings from authorised Falowen learning materials, your self-reported recall outcomes, review streaks, review timestamps and next review dates. It stores up to 100 recently reviewed words per programme and level and up to 20 recent request identifiers and payload hashes for safe retries. Entries older than 180 days are excluded from active review and pruned on a subsequent save; this is not a guarantee of automatic physical deletion after 180 days. Saved vocabulary is separate from official marks. You can continue using read-only access without granting this optional permission.

Optional official coursework submissions

Read-only access cannot submit assignments. If you explicitly grant falowen:assignments:write, confirm a complete final answer, and the limited Falowen A1 pilot is active, ChatGPT may create the first official submission for one of your current A1 tutor assignments. This writes your reviewed answer to the same Falowen submission and lock records as Campus. Your first confirmed submission is final unless you use Falowen's separate resubmission process. Tutor assessment and result publishing remain authoritative in Falowen, not ChatGPT. Where the expanded submission feature is enabled, eligible German A2/B1 and supported Kiswahili A1 untimed workbooks may also be submitted after you review and explicitly confirm your own complete answers. The service stores the submitted writing and relevant reading or listening answers with the account and assignment identifiers, submission timestamp and review status in the existing coursework records. Submission is optional; unsupported tasks, resubmissions and timed mock exams remain in Campus.

What it cannot do

This connection does not take payments, view your billing records, change grades, delete student records, or start examinations. Official submissions are never automatically graded or certified by ChatGPT.

Data handling and authentication

Falowen uses its existing Firebase student-account authentication and a secure OAuth connection to ChatGPT. Authorization codes and access/refresh tokens are stored in restricted Firestore records as cryptographic hashes, expire according to the service's security configuration, and refresh tokens are rotated. Falowen processes the account data needed to answer the request. These records support course access, tutoring, revision, submission processing and account security. Falowen uses Firebase/Firestore for account and learning records, and Google Sheets for existing tutor-score records. The data returned by the tools is limited to the connected account's authorised information. No new sale of student data is authorised by this connection.

ChatGPT as a separate service

Information returned to ChatGPT is also processed by OpenAI subject to the terms, privacy policies and settings applicable to your ChatGPT account. ChatGPT may generate explanations, practice questions and corrections; these generated answers may contain errors. Check official course records in Falowen Campus.

Your choice and access

You can choose not to connect Falowen. If you want to stop using the connection, disconnect the Falowen plugin in ChatGPT's plugin settings. This stops subsequent use of that ChatGPT connection; it does not automatically erase records held in Falowen Campus or prior ChatGPT conversations. For access, correction or deletion requests, including saved vocabulary and practice notes, use the account deletion option in Falowen Campus or contact Falowen through Falowen Help.
