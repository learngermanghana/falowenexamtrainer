Falowen Learning Companion — Privacy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Falowen Learning Companion connects your own Falowen student account to ChatGPT when you choose to authorize the connection. It is operated by Learn Language Education Academy (Falowen), Ghana. The connection is optional.

What the connection may access

After you sign in to Falowen and grant permission, the read-only connection can access your name, enrolled German course level, lesson titles and selected learner-facing course material, assignment list and submission links, recorded tutor feedback and scores, and your assigned live-class schedule, topic and class Zoom link where one is configured. These details are returned to ChatGPT only in response to relevant tool requests. It cannot view another learner's account through your connection.

What it cannot do

This connection does not take payments, view your billing records, change grades, submit tutor-marked assignments, delete student records, or start examinations. All official assignment submission and marking remain in Falowen Campus.

Data handling and authentication

Falowen uses its existing Firebase student-account authentication and a secure OAuth connection to ChatGPT. Authorization codes and access/refresh tokens are stored in restricted Firestore records as cryptographic hashes, expire according to the service's security configuration, and refresh tokens are rotated. Falowen processes the account data needed to answer the request. No new sale of student data is authorised by this connection.

ChatGPT as a separate service

Information returned to ChatGPT is also processed by OpenAI subject to the terms, privacy policies and settings applicable to your ChatGPT account. ChatGPT may generate explanations, practice questions and corrections; these generated answers may contain errors. Check official course records in Falowen Campus.

Your choice and access

You can choose not to connect Falowen. If you want to stop using the connection, disconnect the Falowen plugin in ChatGPT's plugin settings. This stops subsequent use of that ChatGPT connection; it does not automatically erase records held in Falowen Campus or prior ChatGPT conversations. For questions about access, correction or deletion of records, contact Falowen through Falowen Help.
