Frequently asked questions

Can I learn Swahili online in Ghana?

Yes. Falowen provides online beginner Kiswahili learning materials accessible from Ghana and other countries.

Is this course suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. The A1 learning path introduces essential greetings, vocabulary, everyday dialogue and basic language structures.

Is Swahili the same as Kiswahili?

Yes. Kiswahili is the name used in the language itself; Swahili is the common English name.

Does Falowen also teach German?

Yes. Falowen offers German courses from A1 to C2 alongside its Kiswahili programme.
