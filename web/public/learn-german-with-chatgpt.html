How to connect Falowen to ChatGPT

  1. Have a Falowen student account. Register or sign in to your existing account.
  2. Open ChatGPT. Find Plugins or connected apps and search for Falowen Learning Companion, if available to your account or workspace.
  3. Connect and authorize. Follow the Falowen sign-in and consent prompts. Only connect through an authorized Falowen sign-in page.
  4. Ask a question. Start with “Open my Falowen and show my next assignment.” Your own authorized records will be used where supported.

If Falowen Learning Companion is not listed, it may not yet be available in your ChatGPT account. You can continue your course normally in Falowen Campus. Do not share your password in a chat message.

What the Falowen Learning Companion can help with

Assignments and progress

Look up available tutor assignments and recorded outcomes for your own account. Missing scores do not necessarily mean an assignment was not submitted.

Lessons and class preparation

Ask for an enrolled lesson topic and available learning material. If your verified class timetable is available, the companion can help you prepare for the next scheduled German lesson.

A1–C2 German practice

Falowen covers German levels A1 through C2. Companion features and the amount of lesson content available vary by level and currently published connection. Some advanced lessons are for independent study and do not have verified tutor-marked completion.

Official work stays in Falowen

Continue writing and submitting your official workbooks, taking timed mocks, attending classes and receiving official scores in Falowen Campus. The ChatGPT connection is a study aid, not a replacement for tutor marking.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Falowen with ChatGPT?

Eligible students can use the Falowen Learning Companion when the connection is available to their ChatGPT account or workspace and they authorize access to Falowen.

Does ChatGPT know my grades automatically?

No. It can only retrieve supported records when you connect and authorize the Falowen Learning Companion. It should not invent missing grades or class schedules.

Can ChatGPT submit my workbook or change my score?

No. Official assessment submission, marking and grading continue through Falowen Campus.

Does this work for Swahili too?

At present, the connected course-content tools focus on German. Do not assume Swahili lessons are available through the companion.

Is the companion an official Goethe exam service?

No. Falowen provides German learning and exam-style practice; it is not an official Goethe examination body.
